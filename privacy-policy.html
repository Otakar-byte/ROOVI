Privacy Policy

ROOVI · last updated October 29, 2026

Your body is not content.

Camera

The camera is used only as a motion sensor to recognize your body and hands and count repetitions. All processing happens on your phone. Camera images and video are never saved and never sent anywhere.

What is stored

Only workout results (exercises, counts, times) and your settings are stored – on your phone. ROOVI has no accounts, no ads and no user tracking.

iCloud (optional)

If you turn on sync, your workout results are synchronized through your own iCloud account. We have no access to them.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow Apple Health, ROOVI saves completed workouts (activity type and time) to the Health app. ROOVI does not read any data from Health.

Backups

You create backups yourself and decide where to save them or whom to send them to.

Third-party technology

Body recognition uses the Google ML Kit library on your device. It may send anonymous technical performance data – never images. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; we do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete workouts in Progress (trash icon, or swipe a workout to the left) or everything in Settings → Privacy & data → Delete entire history. Deleting the app removes all data from your phone.
